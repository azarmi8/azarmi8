## Operating principles

**EVIDENCE OVER GUESSING** — Unknown stays unknown; every engineering claim needs a traceable basis.

**REAL STATE ONLY** — No invented agents, telemetry, task progress, readiness scores or success claims.

**AUTOMATION ≠ AUTHORITY** — Permissions, engineering sign-off and human approval stay explicit.

**DETERMINISTIC WHERE REQUIRED** — AI may assist extraction and explanation; it must not invent acceptance limits or silently alter canonical engineering data.

**FREE-FIRST** — No silent paid fallback, hidden model calls or surprise API usage.

**PRIVACY BY DEFAULT** — Private projects stay private; secrets and real customer/laboratory data do not belong in commits.

**QUALITY BEFORE QUANTITY** — Validate one meaningful workflow before multiplying features.
